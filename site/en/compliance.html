Compliance & Security

Compliance is a primitive — not a plugin.

Masking, audit and human oversight are part of the architecture, not an optional module. You can't turn off what protects the data — and that's a design decision. Built for sensitive data and regulated sectors, in Brazil and Europe.

The pillars

Protection, traceability and control — by construction.

Role-based masking

PII tokenized in the flow (e.g., [id:***-00]); the original value is visible only to authorized roles, and the customer receives only the partial. PIN/password capture in an atomic block — never persisted to log, queue or history.

Audit on every call

Each call to a business system (MCP) produces an immutable record — who, what, when, allowed, injection detected. Policy is defined per tool; the caller cannot turn off the audit.

Native human oversight

Supervisors and reviewers are session participants, with authority to intervene. It's the "human oversight" required by AI regulation for high risk — embedded, not bolted on.

Access control (RBAC + ABAC)

Roles + attributes per module and field + scope per pool and per group/shift. Validated on the front and back end, with graceful degradation for legacy accounts.

Per-participant visibility

By field and by role: a supervisor can follow an interaction without seeing the sensitive data. Sensitive-data delegation with oversight.

No lock-in / sovereignty

BYO LLM + integration only via MCP + logic in versionable YAML = real portability. Configurable data residency — meets vendor substitution and sovereignty.

🔒 How it works underneath: tokens in the session stream keep the masked value and the original separated; the original is released only to authorized roles. Every domain call passes through permission validation → injection guard → audit before reaching any system. RBAC/ABAC travel in the access token.

Regulatory adherence · Brazil

Ready for the Brazilian regulated environment.

The architecture responds directly to the requirements of LGPD and the sector regulators.

LGPD / ANPDPII minimization and masking, role-based visibility and an audited access trail — aligned with the ANPD's priorities (minimization and transparency of automated decisions).
BACEN Res. 4.893The "Brazilian DORA": requires banks' IT suppliers to provide audit and a substitution plan. Native audit + portability (no lock-in) help the institution comply.
ANS RN 623Per-interaction ticket, access to recordings and traceability — covered by the session trace, audit and quality evaluation.
ANATEL (RGC)Humanized service, deadlines and ombudsman — native human oversight and traceability of the process history.
PL 2338 (AI Framework)Risk-based, with mandatory human oversight for high risk. The orchestration Console anticipates the requirement. (In progress — preparation, not yet an obligation.)

Regulatory adherence · Europe

Designed for the European regulatory cycle.

For EU operations, the positioning (oversight + audit + no lock-in + sovereignty) is aligned with what the regulation now requires.

GDPR + EU↔Brazil adequacyThe mutual adequacy (Jan 2026) allows EU↔Brazil flows without extra mechanisms. Masking and minimization meet data-protection principles.
EU AI ActTransparency (declaring it's AI), human oversight and recording/logging of interactions — backed by the session model, the trace and the audit.
DORAApplies to IT suppliers serving financial firms: audit rights, exit strategy and concentration management — native audit + BYO LLM + sovereignty match directly.
Data sovereigntyBYO LLM and agnostic integration allow hosting and inference in an EU sovereign cloud, without a US provider by default.

⚠️ A deliberate design detail: sentiment reading targets the customer, not the worker — the EU AI Act prohibits inferring employee emotion in the workplace. A boundary we respect by design.

Invariants

Guarantees the architecture never breaks.

They're not conventions — they're contracts guaranteed by the platform.

  • Every domain call is intercepted (permission + injection + audit).
  • Audit is defined per tool — the caller cannot opt out.
  • Agents never access business systems directly, only via authorized MCP.
  • The masked original value is never exposed to unauthorized roles.
  • A masked input value (PIN/password) never enters a queue, stream or log.
  • The DPO's access to sessions and calls is recorded in an immutable log.

📋 Certifications: SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are on the roadmap. The architecture already produces the technical evidence that underpins them — audit trails, masking and access control — so certification formalizes what the product already does.
