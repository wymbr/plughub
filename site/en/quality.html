Quality & Evaluation

Let your AI learn and evolve with humans.

Good AI learns from the human team and improves every cycle. PlugHub evaluates each interaction, compares versions of agents — and humans — and ties quality to each deploy, in a continuous-improvement loop. Evaluation isn't an end-of-month report; it's part of the operation.

>40%

of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by 2027 due to immaturity and lack of proven value (Gartner, 2025). The differentiator isn't having AI — it's having AI that learns, measurable and governed. Whoever doesn't measure and evolve, doesn't scale.

Continuous evaluation

Campaigns evaluate the interaction — by AI and by human.

You define forms with criteria, and campaigns decide what, how much and when to evaluate. Evaluation runs over the data the platform already produces, from day one.

Forms with criteria

Objective and subjective indices, broken down by dimension:

  • Objective: times, resolution
  • Subjective: behavior, knowledge, agility, adherence, resolution support

Configurable campaigns

Per pool and per period, you define:

  • Which form to use
  • Continuous or closed-period evaluation
  • % of contacts evaluated per agent
  • Processing schedule

AI evaluator

Scores each criterion with evidence, backed by a knowledge base (RAG) — it doesn't give a blind score, it shows why. Scales to 100% of contacts when you want.

Human evaluator

The quality team evaluates and reviews with the same form, on the same dataset — humans and AI coexist in the evaluation process, not in separate tools.

Agent Workbench

360° comparison — human × AI, version × version.

The Workbench is where management happens: compare agents side by side and tie each metric to the deploy that produced it. It's real QM (quality management) — but correlated to the versions of your AI agents.

The 360° of quality

Don't trust a single score blindly — not even the AI's.

A quality score isn't absolute truth. That's why quality is triangulated by three points of view that check each other: the operation evaluates, governance supervises the evaluator, and the customer gives the reality test.

① AI evaluator — the score

The operation's view: scores each criterion with evidence and scales to 100% of contacts. But it's an opinion — to be supervised, not treated as absolute truth.

② AI + human reviewer — the check

Governance's view: the reviewer supervises the evaluator and generates the signal that makes it evolve (calibration). It's the "who evaluates the evaluator" — with the final decision always human.

③ NPS — the customer's voice

The view of the person who was served. A high internal score is useless if the customer left unsatisfied: NPS is the reality test that closes the 360°.

🎯 It's the Workbench cross-cut that puts the three side by side and raises the alert when they disagree — for example, high evaluation but low NPS (perception gap), or "marked resolved" with a low score. Divergence is where management acts.

Session replay

Review any interaction — and compare versions before promoting.

Deterministic replay of closed sessions, with a comparison and diff mode turn by turn (similarity, divergences, outcome change, sentiment, latency). It's the basis for evaluation, case reproduction and regression before pushing a new version to production.

🔒 All of this respects role-based masking and the audit trail — quality and compliance in the same place. Meets traceability requirements of regulated sectors (e.g., healthcare regulators in Brazil; human oversight required by the EU AI Act).
