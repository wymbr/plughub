Quality & Evaluation

Let your AI learn and grow alongside your people.

Good AI learns from the human team and gets better every cycle. PlugHub scores every interaction, compares agent and human versions, and ties quality back to each deploy — a continuous-improvement loop. Evaluation isn't an end-of-month report. It's part of the operation.

Continuous evaluation

Campaigns evaluate your interactions — by AI and by humans.

You define the forms; campaigns decide what to evaluate, how much, and when. It all runs on the data the platform already produces — from day one.

Forms with criteria

Objective and subjective indices, broken down by dimension:

  • Objective: times, resolution
  • Subjective: behavior, knowledge, agility, adherence, resolution support

Configurable campaigns

Per pool and per period, you define:

  • Which form to use
  • Continuous or closed-period evaluation
  • % of contacts evaluated per agent
  • Processing schedule

AI evaluator

Scores each criterion with evidence, backed by a knowledge base (RAG) — no black-box number, it shows the reasoning. Scale it to 100% of contacts whenever you want.

Human evaluator

The quality team evaluates and reviews with the same form, on the same dataset — humans and AI work in one evaluation process, not in separate tools.

The 360° of quality

Don't trust a single score blindly — not even the AI's.

No quality score is the whole truth. So quality is triangulated across three vantage points that keep each other honest: the operation scores, governance watches the evaluator, and the customer delivers the reality check.

① AI evaluator — the score

The operation's view: it scores every criterion with evidence and scales to 100% of contacts. But it's still an opinion — one to supervise, not take as gospel.

② AI + human reviewer — the check

Governance's view: the reviewer checks the evaluator and produces the signal that makes it better (calibration). This is who evaluates the evaluator — with the final call always human.

③ NPS — the customer's voice

The view from the other end of the line. A glowing internal score means nothing if the customer walked away unhappy — NPS is the reality check that closes the loop.

🎯 The Workbench cross-cut is what lines the three up and flags the moments they disagree — say, a high evaluation but low NPS (a perception gap), or "marked resolved" on a low-scoring contact. Disagreement is where managers step in.

Session replay

Review any interaction — and compare versions before promoting.

Deterministic replay of closed sessions, with turn-by-turn comparison and diff (similarity, divergences, outcome changes, sentiment, latency). It's the foundation for evaluation, reproducing cases, and regression testing before a new version goes to production.

🔒 All of this respects role-based masking and the audit trail — quality and compliance in the same place. Meets traceability requirements of regulated sectors (e.g., healthcare regulators in Brazil; human oversight required by the EU AI Act).
