How it works

Every contact is a conference room — with an orchestrator in control.

Each session has an orchestrator (AI or human) leading it, and specialists and supervisors join and leave as needed — all as equal participants. The business process is channel-abstract; channels plug in underneath. A three-level model keeps the logic independent of where the customer talks — start on one channel and continue on another, preserving context.

a

Business flow (channel-abstract)

Orchestrates agents and sub-flows, persists via suspend/resume. Never touches the channel.

↕
b

Channel access

Reconciles channel switching and runs each interaction episode.

↕
c

I/O agent on the channel

Renders in each channel's native format (buttons, voice/DTMF, cards). Human or AI.

⚙️ Under the hood: a declarative flow interpreter (Skill Flow) with 13+ step types, state persisted on every transition, and the Routing Engine as the sole arbiter of allocation (SLA, skill, availability and performance).

The thread is never lost — interactions linked by construction

The customer's process is the same from start to finish — through drops, waits and channel switches. Interactions are born linked by the same process identifier and the segment trace, so you never have to hunt for and correlate contacts again: the process history comes pre-assembled, from first touch to outcome. It's what the old "Journey" promised — now by construction, not a separate module.

Smart routing

The orchestrator decides who handles what — by profile and by SLA.

The Routing Engine is the sole arbiter of allocation. Each contact goes to the right resource by combining several criteria — and the queue is evaluated by wait time against the SLA, not by blind arrival order.

By profile — of the agent and the customer

The agent's competency for the skill and the customer's profile (priority, gold, VIP) feed the queue weight. Channel is a hard filter; medium (voice/video/text) fine-tunes the score.

By SLA (wait time)

Urgency grows as the wait approaches the SLA target — the contact rises in the queue before it breaches.

By availability and performance

Load and availability feed the score; optionally, historical performance (more resolution, less escalation) weights the choice.

⚙️ Agent pause and gateway outage are hard filters; ties broken by shortest queue. And the queue can be attended — an AI agent tracks the wait, reports position and time, and offers another channel — not just a silent waiting room.

On the customer's side · Business in Any Media

Start on one channel. Continue on another. Track from any.

For your customer there are no "separate channels" — there's a single process. They start on WhatsApp, get the update by email, finish by voice — without repeating context and picking up where they left off. And they can track progress whenever they want, on the channel they already use.

Switch channels without restarting

The process is channel-abstract and persists (suspend/resume): changing channel doesn't reset context or history.

Resume where it stopped

Connection dropped, a reply pending, came back days later? The process continues at the exact point — nothing is lost.

Track on any channel

"Where's my order/process?" has an answer on the channel the customer prefers — no new app, no memorized ticket number.

Compliance & security

Built for sensitive data and regulated sectors.

Masking, audit and human oversight are part of the architecture — not an optional module. Compliant with data-protection law, and designed for the audit requirements of banks (BACEN) and the European market (EU AI Act / DORA).

Role-based masking

PII tokenized in the flow; the original value is visible only to authorized roles, and the customer receives only the partial. PIN/password capture in an atomic block, never persisted.

Audit on every call

Every call to a business system produces an immutable record (who, what, when, allowed). Native audit trail — the caller cannot turn it off.

Native human oversight

Supervisors and reviewers are session participants, with authority to intervene — exactly the "human oversight" that AI regulation requires for high risk.

No lock-in / sovereignty

BYO LLM + MCP integration + logic in YAML = real portability. Meets vendor-substitution and data-residency requirements.

Quality & evaluation

Let your AI learn and evolve with humans.

Good AI doesn't arrive finished — it learns from the human team and improves every cycle. PlugHub evaluates each interaction, compares versions of agents (and humans) and ties quality to each deploy, in a continuous-improvement loop — over the data your own operation produces, with an orchestration Console and real-time sentiment.

Continuous evaluation by campaigns

Contestation & review

360° comparison workbench

Session replay + diff

Review any session and compare versions before promoting — the basis for regression and continuous calibration.
