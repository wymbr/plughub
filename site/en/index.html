How it works

Every contact is a conference room — with someone in charge.

Every session has an orchestrator — AI or human — running it, while specialists and supervisors come and go as needed, all equal participants. The business process is channel-abstract; channels plug in underneath. A three-level model keeps your logic independent of where the customer is — start on one channel, continue on another, context intact.

a

Business flow (channel-abstract)

Orchestrates agents and sub-flows, persists via suspend/resume. Never touches the channel.

↕
b

Channel access

Reconciles channel switching and runs each interaction episode.

↕
c

I/O agent on the channel

Renders in each channel's native format (buttons, voice/DTMF, cards). Human or AI.

⚙️ Under the hood: a declarative flow interpreter (Skill Flow) with 13+ step types, state persisted on every transition, and the Routing Engine as the sole arbiter of allocation (SLA, skill, availability and performance).

The thread never breaks — interactions linked by design

The process stays whole from start to finish — through dropped calls, long waits, channel switches. Everything ties back to the same process identifier and segment trace, so PlugHub connects the dots for you — no tracking down and matching contacts by hand. The full history is there, first touch to outcome. It's what the old "Journey" promised — now built in, not a bolt-on module.

Smart routing

The orchestrator decides who handles what — by profile and by SLA.

The Routing Engine is the single arbiter of allocation. Every contact reaches the right resource by weighing several factors at once — and the queue is scored by wait time against the SLA, not blind first-come, first-served.

By profile — agent and customer

The agent's competency for the skill and the customer's tier (priority, gold, VIP) both feed the queue weight. Channel is a hard filter; medium (voice/video/text) fine-tunes the score.

By SLA (wait time)

Urgency climbs as the wait nears the SLA target — a contact moves up the queue before it breaches.

By availability and performance

Load and availability feed the score; optionally, past performance (higher resolution, lower escalation) tips the choice.

⚙️ Agent pause and gateway outage are hard filters; ties go to the shortest queue. And the queue can be attended — an AI agent watches the wait, shares position and time, and offers another channel. Not just a silent holding line.

Compliance & security

Built for sensitive data and regulated sectors.

Masking, audit, and human oversight are part of the architecture — not an optional module. Compliant with data-protection law, and built for the audit demands of banks (BACEN) and the European market (EU AI Act / DORA).

Role-based masking

PII is tokenized in the flow; only authorized roles see the original value, and the customer sees just the partial. PIN/password capture happens in an atomic block and is never stored.

Audit on every call

Every call to a business system leaves an immutable record — who, what, when, allowed. The audit trail is native, and the caller can't switch it off.

Native human oversight

Supervisors and reviewers are session participants with the authority to step in — exactly the "human oversight" that AI regulation requires for high-risk use.

No lock-in / sovereignty

BYO LLM + MCP integration + logic in YAML adds up to real portability. Meets vendor-substitution and data-residency requirements.

Quality & evaluation

Let your AI learn and grow alongside your people.

Good AI doesn't arrive finished — it learns from the human team and gets better every cycle. PlugHub scores every interaction, compares versions of agents (and humans), and ties quality back to each deploy in a continuous-improvement loop — all on the data your own operation already generates, with an orchestration Console and real-time sentiment.

Continuous evaluation by campaigns

Forms with objective and subjective criteria; the AI evaluator scores with evidence (RAG) and the human team reviews — on the same dataset.

Contestation & review

360° comparison workbench

Session replay + diff

Replay any session and compare versions before you promote one — the foundation for regression testing and ongoing calibration.
